A Decade of Teaching — From South Delhi to the USA

Shikha’s journey as a yoga teacher has taken her from the leafy streets of South Delhi to international studios in the United States, where she taught Vinyasa Flow to diverse audiences across cultures. That global experience sharpened her ability to meet every student exactly where they are — and guide them somewhere they didn’t know they could go.

The Yoga Inner Power Approach — Science Meets Wisdom

Most yoga teachers teach poses. Shikha teaches your body. Her background in anatomy means she understands why your lower back hurts, where your tension actually lives, and what your specific body needs — not what a generic class prescribes. Every session at Yoga Inner Power in South Delhi is tailored, thoughtful, and built around you.

What Shikha's Students in South Delhi Say

From cardiologists who came in sceptical and left converted, to software developers who finally got rid of decade-long back pain, to entrepreneurs who found mental clarity on the mat — Shikha’s students in Greater Kailash, Hauz Khas, Vasant Vihar, and across South Delhi consistently describe the same experience: they came for the yoga, and stayed for the transformation.
